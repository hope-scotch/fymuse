# Lime Studio — How to open it (Mac)

Hey! This is our band's click + lighting + cue app. It runs right on your Mac —
nothing to sign up for, no account, no internet needed at the show.

It takes about a minute to set up the **first time**. After that you just
double-click it like any app.

---

## 1. Get the app onto your Mac

You'll receive **Lime Studio-mac.zip** (AirDrop, Google Drive, or wherever).

- Double-click the zip to unzip it. You'll get **Lime Studio** (the app).
- Drag **Lime Studio** into your **Applications** folder. (Optional, but tidy.)

## 2. Open it the first time

Because we built this ourselves (not through Apple's store), the first launch
needs one extra click. This is normal and safe — it's *our* app.

**Do this the first time only:**

1. **Right-click** (or Control-click) the **Lime Studio** app → choose **Open**.
2. A box pops up warning it's from an "unidentified developer." Click **Open**.

That's it — it remembers, and from now on you just double-click like anything else.

> **If right-click → Open doesn't show an "Open" button** (newer macOS does this):
> open the app once (it gets blocked), then go to
> ** → System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click
> **"Open Anyway"** next to the Lime Studio message. Then open the app again.

## 3. Allow the microphone (one time)

When you turn on **Audio** in the app, your Mac asks to use the microphone —
click **OK**. That's what lets the lights react to the music and detect the tempo.
(If you miss it: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone**, turn on
Lime Studio.)

---

## Using it

The app opens in its own window. Up top you've got the **click track** (play/stop,
tempo), and three tabs:

- **Setlist** — our songs; click ▶ on one to load its tempo + settings.
- **Lighting** — the stage lights (works in simulator mode without any gear).
- **Performance** — the big full-screen view for during the show.

You don't need any lights or pedals plugged in to try it — it has a built-in
simulator so you can poke around. If something's confusing, ask in the band chat.

Rock on.
