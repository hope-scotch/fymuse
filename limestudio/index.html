Help & Shortcuts

Everything you need to run a show with Lime Studio.

The three views

Setlist — your songs in order; drag to reorder, tap play to load one. Double-click a song to open the Song editor: a bar timeline where you drop tempo changes (128→144 mid-song, signature switches too), section labels (Verse / Chorus…) and cues that speak automatically when their bar arrives.

Lighting — a live stage preview. Pick a scene (Reactive / Chase / Static / Off), set colour and brightness, patch your fixtures, and connect real DMX (ENTTEC or Art-Net) when you're at the venue.

Performance — the big, glanceable view for during the show: song title, giant BPM, beat dots, and one-tap cue buttons.

The click track

The metronome lives in Performance: tap the metronome button at the bottom of the setlist for a big dial you can drag (or scroll; hold Shift for fine control), set a time signature, or hit Tap and tap along. The big round button starts and stops it. Turn on Audio and Lime Studio listens to the room and shows a detected BPM you can apply — or let Follow chase it live.

Hardware (optional)

Lights — in Lighting → DMX Output, choose ENTTEC (USB dongle) or Art-Net (network), then set each fixture's address in Patch. Everything works in Simulator mode without gear.

MIDI — click the MIDI button up top to map a foot pedal or pad: fire cues, toggle the click, blackout, jump songs — all hands-free. Use Learn, then tap a pad.

Click in your in-ears

Hit Outputs (headphones, top right) and pick your interface's IEM output. The click is rendered straight to that device while backing tracks and spoken cues stay on the default output for front of house. Needs the sounddevice package — it's in requirements.txt, so a normal install already has it.

Backing tracks

Open a song's editor and use + Add audio under the timeline. Set which bar each file starts on and its gain — it plays sample-locked to the click, joins mid-file if you seek, and survives tempo changes. Files are stored in the app's library, so the original can move or be deleted.

Stage lights

In Lighting → DMX Output choose ENTTEC (USB DMX Pro dongle — plug in, pick the port, Connect) or Art-Net (enter the node's IP). Then open Patch and give each fixture its DMX start address and channel layout. Until then, the on-screen simulator mirrors exactly what the rig will do.

Foot pedal / pads

Click MIDI up top, connect your device, then use Learn: tap a pedal, pick what it fires — cues, click on/off, blackout, next song. Mappings save automatically.

Room listening (optional)

The Audio button listens to the room for reactive lighting and live BPM detection. From source this needs the optional mic backend: brew install portaudio then pip install pyaudio (see requirements-optional.txt).

Running from source

In the project folder: python3 -m venv .venv · source .venv/bin/activate · pip install -r requirements.txt · python3 desktop.py. On a corporate network that blocks PyPI, prefix installs with PIP_INDEX_URL=https://pypi.org/simple.

Building the Mac app for the band

./build.sh --public produces dist/Lime Studio.app; ./package-for-band.sh zips it for sharing. Bandmates just unzip and open — first launch needs right-click → Open once (unsigned app), after that it's a normal double-click.

Where your data lives

Everything autosaves to ~/.limestudio — the setlist and settings in show.json, backing tracks in tracks/, rendered voice counts in voice/. Copy that folder to move a show to another Mac.
